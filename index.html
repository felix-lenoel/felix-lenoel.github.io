Félix Lenoël

Predoctoral Researcher, Bocconi University

I work at the intersection of labor economics and public economics, with a specific focus on the effects of minimum wages and on public policies targeting low-wage workers.

I am a predoctoral researcher at Bocconi University, affiliated with the ERC project LIFT-UP, led by Giulia Giupponi. I hold an M.Sc. and a B.A. in Economics from the École Normale Supérieure de Lyon, where I also worked as a research assistant at the Center for Economic Research on Governance, Inequality and Conflict (CERGIC).
